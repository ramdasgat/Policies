Privacy Policy — PageNova

Last updated: 23 August 2026

PageNova ("the app") is developed by Ramdas. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Your documents stay on your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in and no upload. It does show ads, and ads involve Google collecting an advertising identifier — that is the one place data leaves your device without you initiating it.

Your documents

When you add a book, Android's file picker hands the app permission to read that one file. The app reads it to display it, and stores alongside it — on your device only — your reading position, reading time, bookmarks, highlights, notes, tags and the generated cover thumbnail.

None of this is uploaded. There is no server, no cloud sync and no backup service. Uninstalling the app removes it.

On Android 12 and below the app declares READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE so it can reopen files you previously added. It is not declared on Android 13 or newer.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve these, Google collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device information (model, operating system version, language)
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • ad interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked)

This is Google's collection, governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — and specifically https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 for how AdMob uses data.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the app shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and your choice there controls whether ads are personalised. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Removing ads with the one-time purchase stops ad requests entirely.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. The app never sees or stores your payment details — Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether the item is owned. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service.

Feedback

If you use Feedback & Suggestions, the app opens a Google Form in your browser with your message, your device model and the app version filled in. You press submit; the app never sends anything on its own, and you can see and edit every field before you do. Responses reach the developer through Google Forms. If you have no browser, the app instead opens your email app with the same content, and again you choose whether to send it.

Read aloud

Read Aloud passes the text of the page you are on to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Which engine that is, and whether it processes speech on-device or on a server, is determined by that engine and its own privacy policy — commonly Google Speech Services. PageNova does not record audio and has no microphone permission.

Children

PageNova is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
